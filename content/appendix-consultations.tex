\clearpage\section{Weekly consultations}\label{app:consult}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Find a recurring slot.} Agree on a weekly time slot with us and put it in as a recurring MS Teams event, so it does not have to be negotiated every week.

\paragraph{Why every week.} A week is short enough that a wrong turn costs days, not a month, and long enough to have something to show. Come even when little moved: a blocked week is exactly what we can help with.

\paragraph{Why personal.} One-on-one, we can look at your actual work, your code, your draft, your plots, and fix misunderstandings on the spot. Group meetings cannot do that.
\advisortodo{Draft for the empty \enquote{Why personal} note; confirm or rewrite.}

\paragraph{Come with a presentation.} If you arrive with a few slides on what you did this week, or slides that make your work easier for us to understand, we will very much appreciate it, even if an LLM generated them. A good default:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
  \item what you did since the last consultation, with evidence: a figure, a result, a page of text;
  \item what is blocked or where you are unsure;
  \item what you plan for next week;
  \item the questions you need us to decide.
\end{enumerate}
\advisortodo{The four-point default is a draft; confirm or replace it with your list.}

\paragraph{The mirror principle.} At the end of each consultation, say back in your own words what we agreed: the decisions, your next steps, and what we owe you. Then post the same as a short written summary. If your mirror differs from what we meant, we catch it now, not in a week.

\paragraph{Recordings and Teams calls.} Hold the consultation as a Teams call, even if we sit in one room, and record it with transcription. You can then rewatch the details, feed the transcript to your AI tools, and turn it into notes in your repository.
