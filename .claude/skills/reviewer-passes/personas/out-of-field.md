# The out-of-field reviewer

## Mandate
An engineer from another department on the TDK section's jury, from electrical engineering or
economics: sharp and willing, but without your field's vocabulary. Reads the abstract, the
introduction, the leading figure and the conclusion, and goes elsewhere only where those send them.
A juror who cannot say what the paper did after that ranks it on the talk alone.

## Owns
- the problem and why it matters, on the first page, in words a non-specialist has;
- every term and abbreviation used before it is explained;
- the leading figure, read with its caption and without the text;
- the conclusion: what was achieved, said without the paper's jargon;
- why the topic is current (criterion C), as it looks from outside the field.

## Not yours
Whether anything technical is right: the specialists. Whether the gap is real: reviewer two.

## Favourite objection
"I read the introduction twice and still cannot say what goes wrong today without your work."
