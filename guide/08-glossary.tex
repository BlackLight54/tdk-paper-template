\section{Process terms}
{\small
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth} X}
  \caption{Process terms.}\label{tab:glossary-process}\\
  \toprule \textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\ \midrule \endfirsthead
  \toprule \textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\ \midrule \endhead
  \bottomrule \endlastfoot
  Topic manifest & One page approved before the semester: the problem, why it matters, the expected contribution. \\
  Literature review (SLR) & A systematic literature review with logged searches, screening and extraction of each source's findings. It gives the gap claim a documented set of sources. \\
  Gap analysis & Finding what existing approaches cannot do. Produces the positioning figure, gap matrix and a one-sentence gap. \\
  Gap matrix & Requirements compared against approaches in \tplref{ch:related}. The gap appears as the one column meeting every row. \\
  Positioning figure & Related work plotted on two axes with three levels each (\tplref{fig:positioning}). A star marks this work in the cell no existing approach reaches. \\
  Leading figure (vezérábra) & The one figure that explains the work; see \cref{app:writing:figure}. \\
  Spine & One page fixing the paper's claims, terminology and notation; see \cref{app:writing:spine}. \\
  Skeleton mind map & Each chapter planned as paragraph topic sentences with supporting evidence, before drafting prose. \\
  Dependency graph & The skeleton's paragraphs with an arrow to each paragraph that needs them, the contribution in green; see \cref{app:writing:depgraph}. \\
  Beats & What each planned paragraph establishes, its evidence, its use of the example and its link to the next paragraph. \\
  Essentials & The elements every paper of this kind contains, measured by the lint on every compile; see \cref{app:writing:essentials}. \\
  Premortem & Predicting why the paper might score poorly on each criterion, then addressing those objections before drafting. TDK papers receive placements rather than acceptance or rejection. \\
  Anchor & A one-page artefact agreed with us and used to check later work: the topic manifest, spine or full draft; see \cref{app:lifecycle}. \\
  Drift & Work that contradicts an anchor without re-opening it; see \cref{app:lifecycle}. \\
  Official step & A step of the faculty's TDK with a portal date: registration with the abstract, paper upload, the conference; see \cref{app:tdk:dates}. \\
  Department session & The writing session before the deadline and the talk rehearsal before the conference, organised by the department for all its TDK students; see \cref{tab:lifecycle}. \\
  Context upload & Putting our papers, notes and earlier work on the topic into \texttt{notes/} of your notes repository. \\
  Hill climb & Repeatedly assessing work and fixing its weakest point until it is complete, whether proofs, implementation or the paper against the review sheet. \\
  Review sheet hill climb & Repeatedly scoring your paper on the review sheet and fixing the weakest criterion. \\
  Reviewer pass & An agent's reading as a simulated reviewer with a narrow brief, before real review; see \cref{app:lifecycle:reviewers}. \\
  Skim view & The reviewer PDF reduced to headings, labelled items, boxes, figures and captions, with paragraph lengths replacing prose; see \cref{app:lifecycle:reviewers}. \\
  Mirror principle & Advisor time follows student effort, with consistency valued over bursts; see \cref{app:consult}. \\
  Close with a summary & Restating decisions at the end of a consultation and saving a written summary; see \cref{app:consult}. \\
  Last week's checklist & Polish checks, including mechanical lint rules run by \texttt{make check} and paper CI; see \cref{tab:polish}. \\
  Burn-down & Counts of todos, beats, placeholder figures and TODO cells, recorded by paper CI on every push and reduced to zero before submission. \\
  Reviewer PDF & The paper built from \texttt{reviewer.tex}: no notes, no guide; the file to upload. \\
  Results tag & The Git tag and Zenodo version used for the paper's numbers. Name it in the paper and never move it; see \cref{app:zenodo}. \\
  Version DOI, concept DOI & Identifiers for one archived release and for all versions together, respectively. Cite the concept DOI in the paper; see \cref{app:zenodo}. \\
\end{xltabular}
}

\section{Technical terms}
{\small
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth} X}
  \caption{Technical terms from our topics.}\label{tab:glossary-technical}\\
  \toprule \textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\ \midrule \endfirsthead
  \toprule \textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\ \midrule \endhead
  \bottomrule \endlastfoot
  Self-sovereign identity (SSI) & An identity model in which holders keep their own credentials and present them without asking the issuer each time. \\
  Verifiable credential (VC) & A digitally signed statement about a subject, issued by an issuer, held by a holder, and checked by a verifier (W3C standard). \\
  Verifiable data registry (VDR) & The shared registry that issuers, holders and verifiers trust for public data such as schemas, keys and revocation status. \\
  Zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) & A proof that a statement is true which reveals nothing beyond the statement itself. \\
  Hyperledger Fabric & A permissioned blockchain platform for consortia of known organisations. \\
  BPMN & Business Process Model and Notation, the standard notation for business process models. \\
  Tamarin & A prover for security protocols that searches for attacks under a symbolic adversary model. \\
  Lean & A proof assistant and programming language for machine-checked proofs. \\
  Refinery & A tool for partial modelling and graph generation over metamodels. \\
  Liveness, safety & A safety property says nothing bad ever happens; a liveness property says something good eventually happens. \\
  TOCTOU & Time-of-check to time-of-use: a race between checking a condition and acting on it. \\
\end{xltabular}
}
