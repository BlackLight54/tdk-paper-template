\chapter{TDK essentials}\label{app:tdk}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you are working on a hard topic, show your hard work in a TDK paper: the Students' Scientific Conference (Tudományos Diákköri Konferencia) is where a semester of research turns into a reviewed, presented result.

\section{Where to look}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \href{https://tdk.bme.hu/}{BME TDK portal} and the \href{https://tdk.bme.hu/conference/VIK/2026}{faculty's page for the current year}: check the call, the templates, the FAQ, and the review sheet. Calls change from year to year.
  \item The research group's own guide: \href{https://github.com/ftsrg/cheat-sheets/wiki/TDK-Howto}{TDK Howto} in the FTSRG cheat-sheets wiki.
  \item Past first-place papers of the research group and of neighbouring groups at the faculty (\cref{app:tdk:papers}), for structure and tone.
\end{itemize}

\section{Official dates}\label{app:tdk:dates}
The portal publishes the dates each year; these are the 2026 ones, and \cref{tab:lifecycle} plans the semester backwards from them.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{1--30 September:} registration on the portal, with the one-page summary in Hungarian and English. Without it there is no paper.
  \item \textbf{1 October -- 1 November:} paper upload on the portal. The deadline is the last day; the portal closes.
  \item \textbf{2--16 November:} the reviewers read and score the paper. The code can still be polished in this window (\cref{app:zenodo:versions}).
  \item \textbf{18 November:} the conference. A plenary at 8:00, section talks from 8:30, fifteen minutes of talk and five of questions each; the awards ceremony is the same evening.
\end{itemize}

\section{Form requirements}\label{app:tdk:form}
From the faculty call; the template already meets them.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Length}: 30--35 pages of main text. The title page, the optional task description, the table of contents, the bibliography, the abstract and the appendices do not count.
  \item \textbf{Layout}, whether you use \LaTeX{} or Word: A4 paper, single column, Times New Roman or another readable serif font, 12\,pt, single line spacing, margins between 1.5 and 3.5\,cm on every side.
  \item \textbf{Figures and tables} are part of the main text, so they count towards the page limit. Their size is free, but they must be readable in print: avoid text below 10\,pt, labels included.
  \item The faculty's templates under \emph{Letöltések} are diploma thesis templates, so they need small changes for the TDK, such as margins and headings. This template has them.
\end{itemize}

\section{The review sheet}\label{app:tdk:sheet}
Reviewers score the paper on the review sheet (bírálói lap), saved in \texttt{docs/} of this project, 35 points in five criteria. The todo at the start of \tplref{chap:intro} explains the four things to argue throughout: relevance, non-triviality, novelty and correctness; each chapter's todo quotes the criterion that chapter carries, in the sheet's own words, so that you can score yourself against the same sentences the reviewer reads. The top score of each criterion, in the sheet's words:
\begin{description}[noitemsep]
  \item[A. Szerkesztés, stílus (0--5)] \enquote{4--5: kiváló (ha a dolgozat közel hibamentes, jól tagolt, követhető, gördülékeny stílusú)}. The sheet also says: \enquote{Terjedelmi korlát 30--35 oldal. Ettől eltérés esetén 3 oldalanként 1 pont levonás javasolt.}
  \item[B/1. Ábrák, táblázatok] \enquote{4: kiváló (a dolgozat megfelelő számú és jól átgondolt ábrát, táblázatot tartalmaz, és a szakirodalmi hivatkozások pontosak)}.
  \item[B/2. Hivatkozások, irodalom feldolgozás] \enquote{3--4: újszerű ipari megoldások és kutatási eredmények (a dolgozat -- az újszerű ipari megoldásokon túl -- bemutatja a dolgozat témakörében releváns kutatási eredményeket a szakterületen)}. B is the average of B/1 and B/2.
  \item[C. A dolgozat témája (0--8)] \enquote{7--8: tudományos vizsgálat szükséges (ha a dolgozat témája korszerű, nem lezárt, vizsgálata magas szintű, tudományos, elmélyült tudást igényel)}.
  \item[D/1. Tudományos újdonságtartalom és kontribúció (0--5)] \enquote{5: újszerű (legalább hazánkban újszerű, vagy eddig még nem létező megoldás)}; one step below, \enquote{3--4: inkrementális (értékes inkrementális kontribúció, létező megoldások apró továbbfejlesztése)}.
  \item[D/2. Technikai helyesség és színvonal (0--5)] \enquote{5: kiváló (kimagasló színvonalú kidolgozás, a közölt eredmények helyessége kétségkívül megalapozott)}. D is the sum of D/1 and D/2.
  \item[E. Bevezetés és az eredmények értékelése (0--8)] \enquote{7--8: teljeskörű -- A bevezetés megfelelő és az elért eredmények pontosak és teljesek, az értékelés megalapozott. A szerző skálázhatósági, robosztussági vizsgálatokat is elvégzett, mellyel igazolta, hogy rendszere ipari környezetben is megállja a helyét}.
\end{description}

\section{A thesis from this paper}\label{app:tdk:thesis}
A TDK paper often grows into a BSc or MSc thesis, so the template keeps what a thesis needs, commented out in \texttt{thesis.tex}: the thesis title page (\texttt{include/titlepage.tex}), the student's declaration (\texttt{include/declaration.tex}), the faculty's structure guide (\texttt{include/guideline.tex}) and the task description page (\texttt{include/project.tex}). Switch \texttt{\textbackslash vikdoktipus} to \texttt{\textbackslash bsc} or \texttt{\textbackslash msc}, and change what differs from the TDK; from the faculty's own guide, that is:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Length.} A BSc thesis is around 30--45 pages, an MSc thesis 60--90 pages of useful content; ask us, departments differ.
  \item \textbf{Language.} The faculty's default is Hungarian. English is for students who aim at international research or industry, and it costs more in writing and in reading for the advisor and the reviewer. This template is English-only; a Hungarian thesis starts from the faculty's template.
  \item \textbf{Format.} A4, 2.5\,cm margins plus 1\,cm for binding, 11--12\,pt serif, one-and-a-half line spacing. The TDK's 15\,mm margins and single spacing are set in \texttt{thesis.tex}; change them there. Typography beyond that: \citet{Jeney}.
  \item \textbf{Structure.} The faculty's list is in \texttt{include/guideline.tex}: task description, title page, table of contents, declaration, abstracts in both languages, introduction, analysis of the task, prior work, the design with its alternatives, evaluation, acknowledgements, bibliography, appendices. The declaration is mandatory; without it the thesis is not reviewed. The PDF may be at most 15\,MB. In print, the declaration signed by you and the topic description signed by the head of the department (downloaded from the \href{https://diplomaterv.vik.bme.hu/}{dipterv portal}) must be included, which is easy to forget.
\end{itemize}
