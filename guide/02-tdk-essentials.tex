\chapter{TDK essentials}\label{app:tdk}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Present your research in a TDK paper at the \href{https://tdk.bme.hu/}{Students' Scientific Conference} (Tudományos Diákköri Konferencia; \href{https://tdk.bme.hu/conference/VIK/2026}{this year's faculty call}). The conference gives a semester's work a written result, a review and a presentation. The best papers qualify for the national round, the \href{https://otdk.hu/}{OTDK}.

\section{Where to look}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \href{https://tdk.bme.hu/}{BME TDK portal} and the \href{https://tdk.bme.hu/conference/VIK/2026}{faculty's page for the current year}: check the call, the templates, the FAQ, and the review sheet. Calls change from year to year.
  \item The group's \href{https://github.com/ftsrg/cheat-sheets/wiki/TDK-Howto}{TDK Howto} covers portal steps, the slide template and presentation advice. Its chapter page ranges predate the call's 30--35 page limit. Follow the call.

  \item Past first-place papers of the research group and of neighbouring groups at the faculty (\cref{app:tdk:papers}), for structure and tone.
\end{itemize}

\section{Official dates}\label{app:tdk:dates}
The portal publishes dates each year. These are the 2026 dates, shown in \cref{fig:timeline}; \cref{tab:lifecycle} plans the semester backwards from them.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{1--30 September:} register on the portal with Hungarian and English abstracts (the paper's Kivonat and Abstract). At this stage, they describe the planned work (\cref{app:writing:abstract}). Registration is required to submit a paper.
  \item \textbf{1 October -- 1 November:} upload the paper. The portal closes on the last day.
  \item \textbf{2--16 November:} the reviewers read and score the paper. The code can still be polished in this window (\cref{app:zenodo:versions}).
  \item \textbf{18 November:} conference day. The plenary starts at 8:00 and section talks at 8:30. Each talk has fifteen minutes plus five for questions. The awards ceremony is that evening.
  \item \textbf{Placements.} Each section awards several first, second and third places, plus special prizes. The jury ranks papers using both reviews and talks, so prepare both carefully. First and second places are nominated to the OTDK, held every two years; until recently, only first places qualified. A placement belongs on your CV and counts towards scholarship and doctoral-school applications.
  \advisortodo{Placements: confirm the current nomination rule (first and second place) against this year's call, and add what else a placement brings that students ask about: the prize money or company awards of the section, and how the faculty's own awards relate to the TDK placements.}
\end{itemize}

\section{Form requirements}\label{app:tdk:form}
These are the faculty call's requirements, already reflected in the template.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Length}: 30--35 pages of main text. The title page, the optional task description, the table of contents, the bibliography, the abstract and the appendices do not count.
  \item \textbf{Layout}, whether you use \LaTeX{} or Word: A4 paper, single column, Times New Roman or another readable serif font, 12\,pt, single line spacing, margins between 1.5 and 3.5\,cm on every side.
  \item \textbf{Figures and tables} are part of the main text, so they count towards the page limit. Their size is free, but they must be readable in print: avoid text below 10\,pt, labels included.
  \item The faculty's templates under \emph{Letöltések} are diploma thesis templates, so they need small changes for the TDK, such as margins and headings. This template has them.
\end{itemize}

\section{The review sheet}\label{app:tdk:sheet}
The review sheet (bírálói lap), saved in \texttt{docs/}, awards 35 points across five criteria. \Cref{app:chapters} explains how to argue relevance, non-triviality, novelty and correctness throughout the paper. Each section quotes the criterion for that chapter so you can assess your work against the reviewer's wording. The top scores are defined as follows:
\begin{description}[noitemsep]
  \item[A. Szerkesztés, stílus (0--5)] \enquote{4--5: kiváló (ha a dolgozat közel hibamentes, jól tagolt, követhető, gördülékeny stílusú)}. The sheet also says: \enquote{Terjedelmi korlát 30--35 oldal. Ettől eltérés esetén 3 oldalanként 1 pont levonás javasolt.}
  \item[B/1. Ábrák, táblázatok] \enquote{4: kiváló (a dolgozat megfelelő számú és jól átgondolt ábrát, táblázatot tartalmaz, és a szakirodalmi hivatkozások pontosak)}.
  \item[B/2. Hivatkozások, irodalom feldolgozás] \enquote{3--4: újszerű ipari megoldások és kutatási eredmények (a dolgozat -- az újszerű ipari megoldásokon túl -- bemutatja a dolgozat témakörében releváns kutatási eredményeket a szakterületen)}. B is the average of B/1 and B/2.
  \item[C. A dolgozat témája (0--8)] \enquote{7--8: tudományos vizsgálat szükséges (ha a dolgozat témája korszerű, nem lezárt, vizsgálata magas szintű, tudományos, elmélyült tudást igényel)}.
  \item[D/1. Tudományos újdonságtartalom és kontribúció (0--5)] \enquote{5: újszerű (legalább hazánkban újszerű, vagy eddig még nem létező megoldás)}; one step below, \enquote{3--4: inkrementális (értékes inkrementális kontribúció, létező megoldások apró továbbfejlesztése)}.
  \item[D/2. Technikai helyesség és színvonal (0--5)] \enquote{5: kiváló (kimagasló színvonalú kidolgozás, a közölt eredmények helyessége kétségkívül megalapozott)}. D is the sum of D/1 and D/2.
  \item[E. Bevezetés és az eredmények értékelése (0--8)] \enquote{7--8: teljeskörű -- A bevezetés megfelelő és az elért eredmények pontosak és teljesek, az értékelés megalapozott. A szerző skálázhatósági, robosztussági vizsgálatokat is elvégzett, mellyel igazolta, hogy rendszere ipari környezetben is megállja a helyét}.
\end{description}

\section{A thesis from this paper}\label{app:tdk:thesis}
A TDK paper can become a BSc or MSc thesis. The required files are kept as commented inputs in \texttt{thesis.tex}: the thesis title page (\texttt{include/titlepage.tex}), student declaration (\texttt{include/declaration.tex}), faculty structure guide (\texttt{include/guideline.tex}) and task description (\texttt{include/project.tex}). Set \texttt{\textbackslash vikdoktipus} to \texttt{\textbackslash bsc} or \texttt{\textbackslash msc}, then make the following changes from the faculty's guide:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Length.} A BSc thesis is around 30--45 pages, an MSc thesis 60--90 pages of useful content; ask us, departments differ.
  \item \textbf{Language.} The faculty's default is Hungarian. English suits students aiming at international research or industry, but takes more writing effort and more reading effort from advisors and reviewers. This template is English-only. For a Hungarian thesis, use the faculty's template.
  \item \textbf{Format.} A4, 2.5\,cm margins plus 1\,cm for binding, 11--12\,pt serif, one-and-a-half line spacing. The TDK's 15\,mm margins and single spacing are set in \texttt{thesis.tex}; change them there. Typography beyond that: \citet{guide:Jeney}.
  \item \textbf{Structure.} Follow \texttt{include/guideline.tex}: task description, title page, table of contents, declaration, abstracts in both languages, introduction, task analysis, prior work, design and alternatives, evaluation, acknowledgements, bibliography and appendices. A thesis without the mandatory declaration will not be reviewed. The PDF must be at most 15\,MB. The printed copy must include your signed declaration and the topic description signed by the department head, downloaded from the \href{https://diplomaterv.vik.bme.hu/}{dipterv portal}.
\end{itemize}
