\chapter{The TDK lifecycle}\label{app:lifecycle}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plan the phases in \cref{tab:lifecycle} backwards from the official dates in \cref{app:tdk}. Each step has an owner, a completion condition and a link to guidance.
We use these steps to track each student's progress at the weekly consultation (\cref{app:consult}).
Set \texttt{\textbackslash lintphase} in \texttt{thesis.tex} to your phase's name in lower case, from \texttt{topic} to \texttt{submit}; the lint's checks come due by phase (\cref{app:code:paperci}). After the upload, leave it at \texttt{submit}: the paper is submitted and needs no further checks.
Inspect the reviewer PDF every week. It is the version reviewers will read.

Three steps are \anchor{}s: the topic manifest, the spine and the full draft. An anchor is a one-page artefact agreed with us that later work must follow. The topic starts as a direction. The spine fixes its claims and names; the full draft leaves only polishing. You can continue working at an anchor, but work that contradicts it is drift. Revisit the agreement first, or you may have to redo everything built on it.

Red rows mark anchors. Yellow rows marked \official{} are faculty steps with fixed portal dates (\cref{app:tdk:dates}); missing one ends that semester's TDK. Green rows marked \department{} are department sessions for all its TDK students. We organise them to resolve the final problems together.

\section{When to run reviewer passes}\label{app:lifecycle:when-passes}
Run the reviewer passes (\cref{app:lifecycle:reviewers}) at four steps of \cref{tab:lifecycle}: the premortem, the panel pass, the skim pass, and the reviews and questions. Each row says when the step is done.

\section{Last week's checklist}\label{app:lifecycle:polish}
Use the polish phase for checks. Any row that takes more than an hour should have been completed in the draft phase. A rule name in brackets marks a row the lint checks: run the rules with \texttt{make check-reviewer} (\cref{app:code:paperci}), and keep them passing before the last week, as the paper CI runs them on every push. Check the remaining rows by reading.
