\chapter{The TDK lifecycle}\label{app:lifecycle}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A TDK paper comes together in the phases of \cref{tab:lifecycle}, planned backwards from the official dates of \cref{app:tdk}. Each step has an owner, a condition that says when it is done, and a pointer to what helps.
We track every student's progress in the same steps, and the weekly consultation (\cref{app:consult}) is where each step is looked at.

Three steps are \anchor{}s: the topic manifest, the spine and the full draft. An anchor is a one-page artefact that we agree on together and that everything after it is measured against. It is where your topic narrows: before the manifest, the topic is a direction; after the spine, it is a fixed set of claims with fixed names; after the full draft, it is a paper that only gets polished. Nothing stops at an anchor, but work that contradicts one is drift, and drift is the most expensive mistake in the process, because everything built on the old anchor has to be redone.

Anchor rows are shaded red. Rows marked \official{} and shaded yellow are the official steps of the faculty's TDK: the portal dates of \cref{app:tdk:dates} are fixed, and missing one ends the semester's TDK. Rows marked \department{} and shaded green are the department's own sessions: we organise them, every TDK student of the department comes, and they are where the last problems get fixed in company.

\section{Last week's checklist}\label{app:lifecycle:polish}
The polish phase is a checklist, not a phase of writing: if a row needs more than an hour, it belongs to the draft phase and the draft was not full. The mechanical rows are lint rules in \texttt{scripts/lint.lua}, one command each: \texttt{make check} runs them all against the last build, \texttt{make check-reviewer} against the reviewer build, \texttt{make lint-traceability} runs one, and \texttt{texlua scripts/lint.lua list} names them. The paper CI (\cref{app:code:paperci}) runs the same rules on every push, so by the last week they have been green for a while. The rest you check by reading.

  Page count [pages] & The main text, from the first page of \tplref{ch:intro} to the last page of \tplref{ch:conclusion}, is 30--35 pages; appendices, the bibliography, the abstract and the title page do not count. \\
  Build log [build, overfull] & No undefined or multiply defined labels, no undefined citations, no overfull boxes, no missing fonts or files. \\
  Cross-references [figures] & Every reference uses \texttt{\textbackslash cref}; every figure, table, listing and equation is referenced from the text (\cref{app:latex:cref}). \\
  Traceability [traceability] & Every requirement is a row of the gap matrix and of the correctness table, every threat is met by a guarantee, every guarantee is checked in the evaluation, and the conclusion restates every contribution with its evidence. \\
  Outline [outline] & Chapters are renamed where the template says so, no heading is a TODO, the running example is named and appears in every chapter. \\
  Figures are vector & Every figure is TikZ in the paper repository, or has a written reason not to be; a draw.io draft was redrawn or exported as PDF only as a last resort (\cref{app:writing:figure}). \\
  Yours is visible & In the leading figure and every architecture-like figure, every component of yours is in the one blue of the \texttt{ours} style and the caption says so (\cref{app:writing:figure}). \\
  Axes and legends & Every axis has a label with a unit; every legend entry names a thing the text also names. \\
  Captions & Every caption explains the figure in the sense of \cref{app:writing:captions}: a claim first, then context. \\
  Readable in print & No text in a figure is below 10\,pt when printed; colour pairs are distinguishable in greyscale. \\
  Tables & Tables fit the text width, numbers are right-aligned with the same precision in a column, units are in the header. \\
  Spine drift & Every term, symbol and claim matches the spine; one name per concept across chapters. \\
  Citations [bib] & Every claim from the literature is cited; bibliography entries have author, title, venue and year; DOIs where they exist; capitalisation protected with braces (\cref{app:latex:cite}). \\
  Abbreviations, notation, terms [acronyms] & Every abbreviation goes through \texttt{\textbackslash gls}, every symbol of the model has a notation entry, and the three tables contain only what the text uses (\cref{app:latex:acro}). \\
  Front and back matter [placeholders] & Title page variables, abstract in both languages, the AI declaration, acknowledgements and the appendices are filled in; no \enquote{TODO} and no placeholder figure remains. \\
  Notes gone [beats] & Every beat has become prose and every todo and advisor note is resolved; the burn-down counts are zero. \\
  The reviewer PDF & The uploaded PDF is the one \texttt{reviewer.tex} builds (\cref{app:latex:todo}): no note, no list of todos, no guide. \\
  Read-only passes & Three readers who read only one layer still follow the paper: figures and captions alone, the running example boxes alone, and the abstract plus the first paragraph of each chapter alone. Where one of them is lost, that layer is missing a step. \\
  PDF & Bookmarks and metadata are correct, every link resolves, and the Zenodo DOI opens the record. \\
  Spelling and language & A spell check in the paper's language passed; British or American spelling is consistent. \\
  Reproducibility & \texttt{docker run \dots{} reproduce} regenerates every figure and table from the results tag named in the paper (\cref{app:code:readme}). \\
  Review sheet & You scored the paper on criteria A--E yourself, and nothing cheap is left to fix (\cref{app:tdk:sheet}). \\
