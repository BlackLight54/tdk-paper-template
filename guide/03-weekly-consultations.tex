\chapter{Weekly consultations}\label{app:consult}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Find a recurring slot.} Agree on a weekly slot with us and create a recurring MS Teams event.

\paragraph{Why every week.} Weekly meetings leave time to make progress and let us catch problems within days. Come even if little has changed. We can help with a blocked week.

\paragraph{Why one-on-one.} Individual meetings let us focus on your code, draft or plot. We can read it together and ask questions until we understand the problem. They also give us room to discuss scope and effort frankly.

\paragraph{Why in person.} A shared whiteboard helps us work through notation, architecture and the leading figure. It is easier to notice uncertainty and discuss changes of scope face to face. Keep a Teams call running for the recording and transcript (see below).

\paragraph{Come with a presentation.} A few slides showing this week's work or explaining something we need to understand are welcome, including LLM-generated slides. A useful outline:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
  \item what you did since the last consultation, with evidence: a figure, a result, a page of text;
  \item what you read: the papers you added to Zotero this week, one sentence each on why they matter;
  \item what is blocked or where you are unsure;
  \item what you plan for next week;
  \item the questions you need us to decide.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Close with a summary.} End by restating what we agreed in your own words: decisions, your next steps and what we owe you. Save a short written summary in \texttt{notes/} of your notes repository. This lets us correct misunderstandings before the next week.

\paragraph{The mirror principle.} Our time follows your effort. Sustained work earns more of our time, contacts and help beyond the paper; putting the topic aside earns less. Struggling is different from disengaging, and we will help you through it. We also value consistency. Modest progress every week lets us build on earlier work more effectively than a brilliant weekend after a month away.

\paragraph{Recordings and Teams calls.} Record and transcribe the Teams call, even when we meet in one room. Use it to revisit details, give context to AI tools and prepare repository notes. We recommend \href{https://github.com/openai/whisper}{Whisper} large-v3-turbo over Teams' built-in transcription for its accuracy and handling of our mixed Hungarian and English speech. Ask us for our transcription skill.
